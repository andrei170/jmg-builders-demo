Stone, done properly

Teesdale homes need builders who know stone

A lot of what we build round Barnard Castle is stone: new houses and bungalows that sit right next to old ones, barns turned into homes, garden walls and boundary walls. Getting the stone, the coursing and the pointing to match is the difference between a building that fits and one that sticks out.

  • New stone and brick houses and bungalows
  • Barn conversions that keep the character of the building
  • Stone boundary walls, garden walls and dry stone walling
  • Roofs, from new trusses to ridge and verge
